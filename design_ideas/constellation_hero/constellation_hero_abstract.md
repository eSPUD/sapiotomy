# The Thinking Constellation — Design Abstract

One persistent visual carries the whole explorable: an abstract node
constellation — glowing points and edges that read as brain-like without being
anatomical. It sits behind every snap-scrolled slide, and its *behavior*, not its
decoration, encodes each idea. The camera moves; the set never changes.

**Two Gears.** A question flies in, one node flashes, an answer pops out — System 1.
Drag the difficulty dial from 2+2 toward 4502×6398 and the direct "blurt" arc
reddens and misses, while a chain of steps lights up to reach the answer. More
steps lit = more inference-time compute.

**The Trace.** That chain, zoomed in, becomes the real reasoning for 350×638; a
teal shortcut skips the work only when the problem is already familiar.

**Match Night.** "Who won last night?" A fan who watched answers instantly; asked
cold, the model reasons into the dark — you can't think your way to a result you
weren't there for. Hand it the box score and the same chain reasons through. Same
model, both times; the difference was familiarity.

**The Frontier.** Training grows a bright, familiar region; reasoning extends
chains from its edge into the unfamiliar, one question at a time — built from
skills trained in or facts handed over. Reasoning can't grow the region; it only
reaches out from the edge.

Restraint is structural: animation fires only when it encodes the point, then
settles to a near-still idle so text always wins. Cool→warm always means compute
spent. Rendered in 2D (d3 + framer-motion), honoring `prefers-reduced-motion` and
the per-page performance budget. *(A fifth slide, The Bill, is drafted but held
out of sequence.)*
