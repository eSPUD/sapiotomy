Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct answered the same 100 hard MATH-500 problems twice. In System 1 the prompt ends with “Give only the final answer. This page is for deciding whether those four are an artifact of the benchmark or a reason to re-run.

Artifact or re-run: what the traces say

Points to noise or the benchmark

  • Four against forty. System 2 wins 40 of the 44 problems where the two disagree (41 of 45 after re-grading). Exact McNemar p < 0.0001 either way.
  • The four don’t reproduce across models. The 1.5B and the 7B both solve number theory 911 in System 2; the 7B solves algebra 2391 in System 2, and the 3B itself solves 2391 with “Let’s think step by step.” appended.
  • Each System 2 miss is one bad step in a sound setup: an addition slip, a misread of unusual notation, an unjustified symmetry assumption, a misremembered formula. Details in each card.
  • System 1’s four answers are short answers to public test problems (3, 10, 2, 3R²). MATH-500 is published, so recall can’t be ruled out; the 1.5B also blurts “2” on intermediate algebra 190.
  • Algebra 2391 writes “(3)5²”, an unusual notation. System 2 read it as 3 × 5 = 15 instead of 3 × 25 = 75, and every step after that follows from the misread.

Points to the run

  • One grading false negative. On intermediate algebra 1981 the model answered (2, 12) \cup (12, 102) and the grader rejected it against (2,12) \cup (12,102): a spacing difference. It hits the 3B and the 7B in System 2 and with the step-by-step prompt. It does not touch the four.
  • Six of the 3B’s System 2 chains end in verbatim loops and hit the 2,048-token cap, a known failure of greedy decoding. Algebra 2391, one of the four, is one of them. None of the six was ever going to finish.
  • One greedy sample per problem and prompt. The run can’t tell a stable failure from a near coin flip on any single problem.

Cheapest checks, in order

  1. Re-grade all 900 traces with whitespace-insensitive comparison. Minutes on a laptop, no GPU. Expected change: +1 for the 3B and the 7B in System 2.
  2. Re-sample only the 44 disagreements at temperature 0.7, five samples per prompt (about 440 generations on the 3B). If System 2 wins most samples on the four, they were draws, not a pattern. Sampling also breaks the greedy loops.
  3. Probe the four for recall: change the numbers in each problem and ask again in System 1. Still right means reasoning; now wrong means the answer was remembered.
